# 1. Scope
This policy applies to the purchase of spare parts and consumables for engine room machinery on all vessels managed by Meridian Fleet Management. Purchases of provisions, crew services and dry-dock contracts follow separate procedures.

# 2. Purchase Orders
Every purchase must be raised as a purchase order in the ShipNet ERP before the supplier is instructed. Verbal orders are only allowed under the emergency procedure in section 6. The purchase order must reference the vessel, the equipment and the catalogue part number.

# 3. Approval Limits
Purchase orders up to USD 5,000 can be approved by the vessel's technical superintendent. Orders above USD 5,000 and up to USD 25,000 need approval from the technical manager. Orders above USD 25,000 need approval from the fleet director. Order values are calculated including any expedite surcharge but excluding freight.

# 4. Quotations
At least three competitive quotations are required for any order above USD 10,000. The requirement can be waived only when the part is available from a single OEM source, and the superintendent must record the single-source justification in ShipNet.

# 5. Preferred Suppliers
Orders for delivery in Singapore should be placed with Poseidon Marine Supply. Orders for delivery in Rotterdam or Antwerp should be placed with NordMarine Parts AS. Orders for delivery in Mumbai should be placed with Konkan Ship Spares. A non-preferred supplier may be used when the preferred supplier cannot meet the required delivery date.

# 6. Emergency Procurement
When a vessel is off-hire or at risk of going off-hire because of a machinery failure, the superintendent may place a verbal order after receiving verbal approval from the technical manager. The purchase order must then be raised in ShipNet within 24 hours. Expedite surcharges are acceptable under this procedure.

# 7. Critical Spares
Spares classed as category A in the catalogue are critical for safe operation. Each vessel must keep at least 2 units on board of every category A spare for its installed equipment. When on-board stock falls below this minimum, a replenishment order must be raised within 7 days.

# 8. Receiving and Inspection
Delivered spares must be inspected within 3 days of delivery on board or at the warehouse. Damaged or incorrect items must be reported to the supplier and to procurement within 48 hours of the inspection.
