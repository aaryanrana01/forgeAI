# 1. Products
Poseidon Marine Supply provides genuine OEM and OEM-approved spare parts for main engines, auxiliary engines, purifiers, compressors and pumps. Every part is delivered with a certificate of origin.

# 2. Prices and Validity
Prices quoted to Meridian are valid for 90 days from the date of the quotation. Catalogue prices are in US dollars and exclude freight.

# 3. Payment Terms
Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. The minimum order value is USD 500 per purchase order.

# 4. Delivery
Standard delivery is to the Poseidon warehouse in Singapore or alongside the vessel in Singapore port within the lead time stated in the catalogue. Delivery alongside the vessel in Singapore is free of charge for orders above USD 5,000; smaller orders are charged a flat delivery fee of USD 150.

# 5. Expedited Dispatch
For urgent orders Poseidon dispatches within 48 hours of the purchase order for a surcharge of 15 percent of the order value.

# 6. Warranty
Parts carry a warranty of 12 months from installation or 18 months from delivery, whichever comes first. Warranty claims must include the part serial number and running hours.

# 7. Returns
Unused parts in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Returns are subject to a restocking fee of 10 percent of the part price.
