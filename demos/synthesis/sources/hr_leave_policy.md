# 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy explains the leave available to shore-based employees on the BlueWake payroll in the Mumbai and Chennai offices. It does not apply to seafarers or other sea staff, whose leave is governed by their Collective Bargaining Agreement and individual employment contracts managed by the Crewing Department.

# 2. Leave Year
The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Entitlements below are stated per leave year and are pro-rated for employees who join or leave during the year.

# 3. Annual Leave
Every shore employee is entitled to 21 working days of annual leave per leave year. Annual leave accrues at 1.75 days for each completed month of service. New joiners may start using accrued annual leave after completing their three-month probation period.
Annual leave of three or more days must be applied for in the BlueDesk HR portal at least 7 calendar days before the first day of leave. The reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 3 working days. Leave taken without approval is treated as unauthorised absence.

# 4. Carry Forward and Encashment
A maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next leave year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March. Employees may request encashment of up to 5 days of annual leave per year, paid at the basic salary rate, by raising a request in BlueDesk during March. On exit, the full accrued annual leave balance is encashed in the final settlement.

# 5. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per leave year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for any absence of more than 2 consecutive days. The employee must inform the reporting manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

# 6. Casual Leave
Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per leave year for personal matters. Casual leave can be taken for a maximum of 2 consecutive days and cannot be combined with annual leave.

# 7. Public Holidays and Compensatory Off
Each office observes 12 public holidays per calendar year. The holiday list for each office location is published by HR in December for the following year. An employee who is asked by the manager to work on a public holiday, including business travel that falls on a public holiday as described in section 6 of the Travel and Expense Policy, earns one compensatory off day. A compensatory off day must be used within 60 days of the holiday worked and must be applied for in BlueDesk.

# 8. Parental Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for the third child onward. Adoption leave for a child below three months of age is 12 weeks. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption of the child.

# 9. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member: spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law.

# 10. Leave Without Pay
Once all other leave is exhausted, an employee may request leave without pay of up to 30 days per leave year. It requires approval from the department head and HR.
