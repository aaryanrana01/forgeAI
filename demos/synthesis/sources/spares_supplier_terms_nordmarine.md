# 1. Products
NordMarine Parts supplies OEM spare parts for turbochargers, main engines, boilers and air compressors, and reconditioned exchange units for turbocharger cartridges.

# 2. Payment Terms
Invoices are payable within 45 days of the invoice date. The minimum order value is USD 1,000 per purchase order.

# 3. Volume Discount
Orders with a value above USD 20,000 receive a discount of 5 percent on the catalogue price.

# 4. Delivery
Delivery is ex works from the NordMarine warehouse in Rotterdam. Delivery to vessels in Rotterdam or Antwerp port is arranged by NordMarine at cost. Lead times in the catalogue are counted in calendar days from the purchase order.

# 5. Expedited Dispatch
Urgent orders are dispatched within 72 hours for a surcharge of 20 percent of the order value.

# 6. Warranty
New parts carry a warranty of 18 months from delivery. Reconditioned exchange units carry a warranty of 12 months from delivery.

# 7. Returns
Unused parts may be returned within 14 days of delivery with a restocking fee of 15 percent. Reconditioned exchange units cannot be returned.
