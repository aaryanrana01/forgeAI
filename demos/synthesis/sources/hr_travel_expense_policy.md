# 1. Scope
This policy covers business travel and expense reimbursement for shore staff. Travel of crew joining or signing off vessels is arranged and paid by the Crewing Department and is not covered here.

# 2. Approval and Booking
Every business trip needs a travel request in BlueDesk approved by the reporting manager before any booking is made. International travel additionally requires approval from the department head. Domestic flights should be booked at least 14 days before travel wherever possible. All bookings are made through the company travel desk.

# 3. Air Travel
Economy class is the standard for all employees. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours and only for employees in grade M3 and above.

# 4. Hotel Limits
The maximum hotel rate per night is INR 7,500 in Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai and Bengaluru) and INR 5,000 in all other Indian cities. For international travel the limit is USD 180 per night, except Singapore where the limit is USD 250 per night.

# 5. Per Diem
The daily allowance for meals and incidentals on domestic trips is INR 1,800 for grades up to M2 and INR 2,500 for grade M3 and above. On international trips the daily allowance is USD 60 for all grades. For day trips shorter than 8 hours, 50 percent of the daily allowance is paid.

# 6. Travel on Weekends and Public Holidays
When business travel falls on a public holiday, the employee is eligible for a compensatory off day as set out in section 7 of the Leave Policy. The holiday travel must be recorded in the travel request so that HR can credit the compensatory off.

# 7. Local Conveyance
Taxi fares for business purposes are reimbursed at actual cost. Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre.

# 8. Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted in BlueDesk within 30 days of the end of the trip. Original receipts are required for every item above INR 500. Claims submitted more than 60 days after the trip are not reimbursed unless the CFO approves them. Approved claims are paid within 10 working days.

# 9. Travel Advance
Employees may request a travel advance of up to 70 percent of the estimated trip cost. The advance must be settled with an expense claim within 15 days of returning.

# 10. Non-Reimbursable Items
The company does not reimburse alcohol, hotel minibar charges, laundry on trips shorter than 4 nights, traffic fines or personal entertainment.

# 11. Contact
Questions about travel bookings go to the travel desk at travel.desk@bluewake.example. Questions about claims go to Finance at expenses@bluewake.example.
