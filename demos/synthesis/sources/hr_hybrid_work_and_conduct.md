title: HYBRID WORK AND CODE OF CONDUCT POLICY
org: BlueWake Shipping Pvt. Ltd. (fictional company for demonstration)
meta: Policy HR-POL-05 | Version 2.1 | Effective 1 July 2025 | Owner: Human Resources

# 1. Eligibility
Hybrid working is open to shore staff whose role is not tied to a site, such as a terminal or warehouse, once they have completed the three-month probation period.

# 2. Hybrid Pattern
Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are in-office anchor days for all teams. Work-from-home days for the coming week must be registered in BlueDesk by the Friday of the previous week.

# 3. Core Hours
Employees working from home must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time.

# 4. Equipment and Allowances
Each eligible employee receives a company laptop and a one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. The allowance is reimbursed against receipts submitted within the first 6 months of becoming eligible, following the normal expense claim rules. An internet allowance of INR 1,000 per month is paid with salary.

# 5. Information Security
Company systems may only be accessed through the company VPN. Working on public Wi-Fi without the VPN is not allowed. A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to IT Security at itsec@bluewake.example within 24 hours.

# 6. Working From Another Location
With manager approval, an employee may work from another city in India for up to 10 working days per year. Working from outside India is not permitted under this policy.

# 7. Gifts and Hospitality
Employees may accept gifts from vendors or customers worth up to INR 2,000. Any gift worth more than INR 2,000 must be declared to Compliance within 5 working days, and Compliance decides whether it may be kept.

# 8. Conflicts of Interest
Every employee must submit an annual conflict of interest declaration in BlueDesk by 30 April.

# 9. Speak-Up and Grievances
Concerns about misconduct can be raised with the manager, with HR, or through the ethics hotline at ethics@bluewake.example. Anonymous reports are accepted. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

# 10. Breaches
Breaches of this policy are handled under the Disciplinary Policy HR-POL-09.
